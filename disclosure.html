Affiliate Disclosure

Plain-language explanation of how this site is funded.

Flat Top Forge participates in affiliate marketing programs, which means some links on this site may earn us a commission when you click through and make a purchase — at no additional cost to you.

What this means

When you click certain product links on this site and buy something, the retailer or brand may pay us a small percentage of that sale. This is standard practice for review and comparison sites, and it's how we keep this site free and ad-light.

What this doesn't mean

Affiliate relationships do not influence which products we choose to compare or how we rank them. If a product is a poor fit for a use case, we say so, regardless of whether it's part of an affiliate program.

Where these links go

Depending on the page, links may go to Amazon, Blackstone Products, or other retailers as those relationships are established. We will keep this page updated as our affiliate partnerships change.
