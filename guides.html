Beginner setup

Think in zones, not one giant temperature

A flat top becomes much easier to manage when part of the surface is used for hard searing, another part for normal cooking, and a lower-heat area for holding food. That lets you move ingredients instead of racing the clock.

Seasoning

Thin layers beat one heavy coat

Seasoning is built gradually. Clean metal, a very thin film of suitable cooking oil, heat, and repetition create a more durable surface than leaving a thick oily layer behind.

Heat control

Preheat, then adjust

Give the cooking surface time to come up to temperature before loading it with food. Once the steel is hot, use burner adjustments and food placement together rather than treating every burner as an on/off switch.

Cleaning

Clean while the cook is still fresh

Remove food debris, scrape the surface appropriately, wipe it down, and leave the cooktop protected before storage. The exact method depends on the griddle and manufacturer instructions, so model-specific guidance should always take priority.
