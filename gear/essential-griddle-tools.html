Essential griddle tools

Buy the tools that solve recurring problems first. The drawer full of novelty gadgets can wait until the spatula situation is no longer tragic.

Start with function, not bundles

Large accessory kits can be convenient, but they often include duplicate or low-use pieces. A smaller set of durable tools usually creates a better working station and makes replacement decisions easier later.

Two thermometers solve different problems

An infrared thermometer estimates surface temperature without touching the griddle. An instant-read probe measures internal food temperature. They are not substitutes for one another, despite both being sold under the magnificently vague label “thermometer.”

What can wait

Specialized egg rings, taco racks, oversized burger presses, warming racks, and other accessories can be useful, but they should come after the basic cook-and-clean workflow is covered.
