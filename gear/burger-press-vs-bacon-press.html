Burger press vs bacon press

Both are heavy objects with handles. Naturally, the cooking world found a way to make that require explanation.

FeatureRound burger pressRectangular bacon press
Best atIndividual smash burgers and compact portionsBacon, sandwiches, multiple pieces, larger contact area
ShapeRoundRectangular or square
FootprintSmaller and easier to position between other foodLarger and better for broad even pressure
Smash burgersExcellent when the base is flat and wide enoughWorks, but can be oversized for single patties
BaconUsable for a few stripsUsually better because it covers more length
Sandwiches / meltsWorks for compact sandwichesBetter for larger bread and even compression

For a basic kit, one good press is enough

If you are buying only one, choose the shape that matches what you cook most. A medium-size flat press with a heat-safe handle can handle burgers, bacon, and sandwiches well enough for most home cooks.

For an upgraded kit, two presses make sense

A round burger press is easier to place over individual patties while a larger rectangular press handles bacon, melts, and broader portions better. That is real functional redundancy rather than collecting cast iron because shelves were feeling emotionally underutilized.

Do not press everything

Pressure is a technique, not a default setting. Smash burgers benefit from an initial hard smash against a hot surface. Many other foods do not benefit from being repeatedly compressed throughout cooking. Use the press when extra contact with the steel improves the result.

What to look for

  • A truly flat bottom that makes even contact.
  • Enough weight to work without excessive force.
  • A handle that keeps your hand away from the hot surface.
  • A size that fits your griddle and normal portions.
  • Construction that can be cleaned easily and maintained according to the manufacturer's instructions.
