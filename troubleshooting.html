Griddle troubleshooting

Start with the symptom and work backward. Random internet rituals are entertaining right up until your cooktop looks worse than when you started.

Common problems
Surface

Rust on the griddle

Judge the severity, remove light oxidation appropriately, rebuild seasoning, and prevent moisture from winning again.

 Seasoning

Sticky or tacky surface

Too much oil, incomplete heating, food residue, and buildup are the most common suspects.

 Performance

Griddle not getting hot

Check preheat, burner settings, weather, fuel supply, regulator guidance, and burner behavior in a safe order.

 Cooking

Food sticking to the surface

Seasoning, temperature, moisture, oil, and timing all matter before the spatula gets convicted.
Troubleshoot systematically

Change one variable at a time

Start with the simplest likely cause, verify it, then move to the next. That prevents a minor cooking or maintenance issue from becoming a full teardown performed because a stranger in a comment section sounded confident.

Safety note: if you smell gas, find damaged hoses or fittings, suspect an electrical fault, see uncontrolled flame, or encounter another hazardous condition, stop using the appliance and follow the manufacturer’s safety instructions or contact a qualified service provider.
