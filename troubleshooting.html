Griddle troubleshooting

When the cooktop is rusty, sticky, uneven, or simply refusing to cooperate, start with the symptom and work backward instead of attacking it with random internet rituals.

Food sticking

Surface condition, temperature, moisture, timing, and oil choice can all matter before the spatula receives the blame.

Rust is a symptom, not a personality trait

Surface moisture, storage conditions, damaged seasoning, and long periods without use can all contribute. Restoration guidance should match the severity of the rust and the manufacturer's instructions for that cooktop.

Sticky seasoning often means too much material was left behind

A heavy oil layer can polymerize unevenly and leave tacky patches. Future dedicated guides will separate simple cleanup from full reseasoning so readers do not strip a usable surface unnecessarily.

Troubleshoot heat systematically

Check basic operating conditions first: proper preheat, burner settings, wind exposure, fuel level, and any manufacturer-listed checks. Gas components should not be modified or disassembled beyond the manufacturer's safe maintenance instructions.

Safety note: for gas leaks, damaged regulators, electrical faults, fire, or other hazardous conditions, stop using the appliance and follow the manufacturer's safety instructions or contact a qualified service provider.
