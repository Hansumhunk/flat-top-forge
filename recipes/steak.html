Steak · Medium · 25 min

Garlic Butter Steak Bites

High-heat seared steak pieces finished with butter, garlic, and herbs.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sirloin or strip steak, cubed
  • 2 tbsp high-heat oil
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Pat steak dry and season.
  2. Heat one zone to high and oil lightly.
  3. Sear steak in a single layer.
  4. Turn once browned and move to a cooler zone.
  5. Add butter and garlic, toss briefly, and serve.

Griddle Cheesesteaks

Thin beef, onions, peppers, and melted cheese packed into toasted rolls.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb shaved beef
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 6 slices provolone
  • 4 hoagie rolls
  • Oil, salt, and pepper

Method

  1. Cook onions and peppers over medium heat until softened.
  2. Turn a second zone to high and sear the shaved beef quickly.
  3. Season and combine with vegetables.
  4. Divide into portions, top with cheese, and melt.
  5. Load into toasted rolls.

Black Pepper Sirloin Strips

Seared sirloin strips with cracked pepper, butter, and onions.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sirloin, thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 1/2 tsp coarse black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to high and oil lightly.
  2. Sear steak in batches so it browns instead of steaming.
  3. Cook onion on a medium zone until softened.
  4. Combine with butter and black pepper.
  5. Serve immediately.

Steak Fajita Platter

Seasoned steak with peppers, onions, lime, and hot tortillas.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb flank or skirt steak, sliced
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 lime
  • 8 tortillas

Method

  1. Season steak and sear over high heat.
  2. Cook peppers and onions beside it over medium-high heat.
  3. Combine when steak is cooked to preference.
  4. Squeeze lime over everything.
  5. Warm tortillas on a cooler zone and serve.

Teriyaki Steak & Vegetables

Quick-seared beef with zucchini, peppers, and a glossy teriyaki finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sirloin, sliced
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 cup teriyaki sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Sesame seeds, optional

Method

  1. Sear steak over high heat and move to a cooler zone.
  2. Cook zucchini and pepper until browned and tender-crisp.
  3. Return steak to the hot zone.
  4. Add teriyaki sauce and toss until reduced and glossy.
  5. Finish with sesame seeds if desired.

Garlic Herb Ribeye

A whole ribeye seared on the flat top and basted with garlic herb butter.

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye steaks, about 1 inch thick
  • 1 tbsp high-heat oil
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp chopped rosemary or thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Pat steaks dry and season generously.
  2. Heat the griddle to high and oil lightly.
  3. Sear 3 to 4 minutes per side, adjusting for thickness and desired doneness.
  4. Move to medium heat and add butter, garlic, and herbs.
  5. Baste briefly, then rest 5 to 10 minutes before slicing.

Steak & Mushroom Melts

Thin steak, buttery mushrooms, Swiss cheese, and toasted sourdough.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb shaved steak
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown mushrooms over medium-high heat.
  2. Sear steak quickly on a hotter zone and season.
  3. Build sandwiches with Swiss, steak, and mushrooms.
  4. Butter the outsides of the bread.
  5. Griddle over medium-low heat until crisp and melted.

Cajun Steak Bites & Peppers

Spicy steak bites with charred peppers and a buttery Cajun finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sirloin, cubed
  • 2 bell peppers, chopped
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt as needed

Method

  1. Toss steak lightly with Cajun seasoning.
  2. Sear over high heat without crowding.
  3. Cook peppers on a medium-high zone until charred.
  4. Combine steak and peppers with butter.
  5. Toss briefly and serve.

Steak & Onion Hoagies

Seared beef, caramelized onions, provolone, and toasted hoagie rolls.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb shaved beef
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 6 slices provolone
  • 4 hoagie rolls
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook onions over medium heat until deeply browned.
  2. Sear shaved beef over high heat and season.
  3. Combine with onions and divide into four piles.
  4. Top with provolone and cover briefly.
  5. Transfer into toasted rolls.

Sweet Chili Steak Bites

Seared steak glazed with sweet chili sauce, lime, and green onion.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sirloin, cubed
  • 1/2 cup sweet chili sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Sear steak over high heat until browned.
  2. Move to medium heat.
  3. Add sweet chili sauce and soy sauce.
  4. Toss until sticky and reduced.
  5. Finish with lime and green onions.

Steakhouse Onion & Blue Cheese Steak

Seared steak topped with griddled onions and tangy blue cheese.

Ingredients

  • 2 strip steaks
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 1/2 cup blue cheese crumbles
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook onions in butter over medium heat until browned.
  2. Season steaks and sear on high heat to preferred doneness.
  3. Move steaks to a cooler zone.
  4. Top with onions and blue cheese.
  5. Cover briefly to soften the cheese, then rest before serving.

Steak & Potato Griddle Hash

Crispy potatoes, steak, onions, and peppers finished together on one surface.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sirloin, cubed
  • 1 lb diced par-cooked potatoes
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, pepper, and garlic powder

Method

  1. Crisp potatoes over medium-high heat in oil.
  2. Add peppers and onions and cook until browned.
  3. Sear steak separately on a hotter zone.
  4. Combine everything when steak reaches preferred doneness.
  5. Season and serve hot.
