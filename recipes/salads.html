Griddle Chicken Caesar Salad

Hot seared chicken over crisp romaine with parmesan and Caesar dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast
  • 2 romaine hearts
  • 1/2 cup parmesan
  • Caesar dressing
  • Croutons
  • Oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder

Method

  1. Season and sear chicken on a medium-high griddle.
  2. Cook through, rest, and slice.
  3. Toss romaine with dressing, parmesan, and croutons.
  4. Top with hot sliced chicken.

Warm Steak & Pepper Salad

Seared steak, peppers, onions, and tomatoes over greens with a simple vinaigrette.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sirloin
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1/2 onion
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Mixed greens
  • Olive oil and vinaigrette

Method

  1. Sear steak to desired doneness and rest.
  2. Cook peppers and onions until browned.
  3. Slice steak thinly.
  4. Arrange warm toppings over greens and finish with vinaigrette.
