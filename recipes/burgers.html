Classic Smash Burgers

Thin, deeply browned patties with crisp edges, melted cheese, and a toasted bun.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional: diced onions, pickles, burger sauce

Method

  1. Preheat the griddle to high heat.
  2. Divide beef into four loose balls.
  3. Place on the hot surface and smash hard for about 10 seconds.
  4. Season, cook until edges brown deeply, scrape, and flip.
  5. Add cheese, toast buns, and assemble.

Oklahoma Onion Smash Burgers

Paper-thin onions smashed directly into beef for a sweet, browned crust.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 1 large yellow onion, shaved thin
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • Salt, pepper, mustard, pickles

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to high.
  2. Place loose beef balls down and pile onions over each.
  3. Smash firmly so onions press into the meat.
  4. Season and cook until deeply browned, then scrape and flip onion-side down.
  5. Add cheese, toast buns, and finish with mustard and pickles.

Bacon Jalapeño Smash Burgers

Smash patties with crisp bacon, seared jalapeños, cheddar, and smoky sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 8 slices bacon
  • 2 jalapeños, sliced
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 4 buns
  • BBQ sauce or burger sauce

Method

  1. Cook bacon over medium heat and reserve.
  2. Sear jalapeños in a little bacon fat.
  3. Smash beef on a high-heat zone, season, and flip once crusted.
  4. Add cheddar and melt.
  5. Build with bacon, jalapeños, and sauce.

Mushroom Swiss Smash Burgers

Hard-seared beef topped with buttery mushrooms and melted Swiss.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  • 4 buns
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown mushrooms in butter over medium-high heat.
  2. Move mushrooms to a cooler zone.
  3. Smash beef on high heat and season.
  4. Flip, top with mushrooms and Swiss, and cover briefly to melt.
  5. Serve on toasted buns.
Burgers · Easy · 20 min

Black & Blue Burgers

Peppery smash patties with blue cheese and caramelized onions.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1/2 cup blue cheese crumbles
  • 4 buns
  • 1 tsp blackening seasoning
  • 1 tbsp butter

Method

  1. Cook onions with butter over medium heat until browned.
  2. Smash beef on high heat and season lightly with blackening seasoning.
  3. Flip when deeply crusted.
  4. Top with blue cheese and onions.
  5. Serve on toasted buns.

BBQ Bacon Cheddar Burgers

Cheddar smash burgers with bacon, griddled onions, and tangy barbecue sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 8 slices bacon
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 1/2 cup BBQ sauce
  • 4 buns

Method

  1. Cook bacon and onions until browned.
  2. Smash beef on high heat and season.
  3. Flip and melt cheddar over each patty.
  4. Toast buns in a little bacon fat.
  5. Assemble with bacon, onions, and BBQ sauce.

Classic Griddle Patty Melts

Beef patties, caramelized onions, Swiss, and rye bread toasted in butter.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 8 slices rye bread
  • 8 slices Swiss cheese
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook onions over medium-low heat until soft and browned.
  2. Form four thin patties and sear over medium-high heat.
  3. Build sandwiches with Swiss, beef, onions, and more Swiss.
  4. Butter the bread outsides.
  5. Griddle until golden on both sides and cheese melts.

Southwest Pepper Jack Burgers

Smash burgers with pepper Jack, grilled peppers, onions, and chipotle mayo.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • 4 slices pepper Jack
  • 4 buns
  • 1/3 cup chipotle mayo

Method

  1. Cook peppers and onions over medium-high heat until browned.
  2. Smash beef on a hot zone and season.
  3. Flip and add pepper Jack.
  4. Toast buns.
  5. Build with vegetables and chipotle mayo.
Burgers · Easy · 20 min

Garlic Butter Steakhouse Burgers

Rich smash burgers finished with garlic butter, provolone, and onions.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 slices provolone
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 buns

Method

  1. Cook onions until browned and tender.
  2. Mix butter and garlic.
  3. Smash beef on high heat, season, and flip.
  4. Add provolone and a small spoon of garlic butter.
  5. Serve with onions on toasted buns.

Breakfast Smash Burgers

Smash patties topped with bacon, fried egg, and American cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 eggs
  • 8 slices bacon
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 buns
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook bacon and reserve.
  2. Smash beef on high heat and season.
  3. Flip, add cheese, and move to a warm zone.
  4. Fry eggs to your preferred doneness.
  5. Assemble burgers with bacon and eggs.
Burgers · Easy · 20 min

Double Mustard Smash Burgers

Thin patties smashed, brushed with mustard, and stacked with pickles and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 8 slices American cheese
  • 1/3 cup yellow mustard
  • 4 buns
  • Pickles and diced onion
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Form eight loose beef balls.
  2. Smash on high heat and season.
  3. Spread a thin layer of mustard on the uncooked side before flipping.
  4. Add cheese and stack two patties per burger.
  5. Finish with pickles and onions.
Burgers · Easy · 20 min

Pizza Smash Burgers

Beef patties with marinara, mozzarella, pepperoni, and toasted garlic buns.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 cup marinara
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 20 pepperoni slices
  • 4 buns
  • 1 tbsp garlic butter

Method

  1. Smash beef on a hot griddle and season.
  2. Flip and top with marinara, mozzarella, and pepperoni.
  3. Cover briefly to melt the cheese.
  4. Toast buns with garlic butter.
  5. Assemble and serve immediately.
