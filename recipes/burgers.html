Classic Smash Burgers

Thin, deeply browned patties with crisp edges, melted cheese, and a toasted bun.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional: diced onions, pickles, burger sauce

Method

  1. Preheat the griddle to high heat.
  2. Divide beef into four loose balls.
  3. Place on the hot surface and smash hard for about 10 seconds.
  4. Season, cook until edges brown deeply, scrape, and flip.
  5. Add cheese, toast buns, and assemble.
Burgers · Easy · 20 min

Oklahoma Onion Smash Burgers

Paper-thin onions smashed directly into beef for a sweet, browned crust.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 1 large yellow onion, shaved thin
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • Salt, pepper, mustard, pickles

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to high.
  2. Place loose beef balls down and pile onions over each.
  3. Smash firmly so onions press into the meat.
  4. Season and cook until deeply browned, then scrape and flip onion-side down.
  5. Add cheese, toast buns, and finish with mustard and pickles.
