Honey Garlic Chicken

Sweet-savory chicken with a sticky griddle glaze.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Method

  1. Mix soy sauce, honey, garlic, and sesame oil.
  2. Cook chicken over medium-high heat until browned and nearly done.
  3. Pour on sauce and keep the chicken moving.
  4. Cook until sauce reduces and coats the chicken.

Griddle Chicken Fajitas

Seared chicken strips with peppers and onions, ready for tortillas.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken breast or thighs, sliced
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 8 tortillas

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high.
  2. Cook chicken with oil and seasoning until browned.
  3. Add peppers and onions beside the chicken.
  4. Combine when vegetables are tender-crisp and chicken is cooked through.
  5. Warm tortillas and serve.

Teriyaki Chicken Bowls

Juicy chicken glazed with teriyaki and paired with griddled vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, chopped
  • 1/2 cup teriyaki sauce
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Cooked rice for serving

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and oil lightly.
  2. Sear chicken until browned on multiple sides.
  3. Cook zucchini and pepper beside it until tender-crisp.
  4. Add teriyaki sauce and toss until glossy and reduced.
  5. Serve over rice.

Lemon Pepper Chicken

Bright, buttery chicken with lemon, cracked pepper, and garlic.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken breast, sliced thin
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp coarse black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Season chicken with garlic powder, pepper, and salt.
  2. Sear over medium-high heat until browned and cooked through.
  3. Lower heat slightly and add butter.
  4. Squeeze lemon over the chicken and toss for 30 seconds.

Buffalo Chicken Cheesesteaks

Chopped chicken, peppers, onions, buffalo sauce, and melted provolone in toasted rolls.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken, thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 cup buffalo sauce
  • 6 slices provolone
  • 4 hoagie rolls

Method

  1. Cook peppers and onions over medium heat until softened.
  2. Sear chicken on a hotter zone and chop as it cooks.
  3. Combine with vegetables and buffalo sauce.
  4. Divide into portions, top with provolone, and melt.
  5. Load into toasted rolls.

Garlic Parmesan Chicken Bites

Crisp-edged chicken bites finished with garlic butter and Parmesan.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken breast, cubed
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Pat chicken dry and season.
  2. Sear in a single layer over medium-high heat until browned.
  3. Move to a slightly cooler zone and add butter and garlic.
  4. Toss for 30 seconds, then add Parmesan.
  5. Serve immediately.

Chicken Bacon Ranch Quesadillas

Griddled tortillas stuffed with chicken, bacon, cheddar, and ranch.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cooked chopped chicken
  • 6 slices bacon, cooked
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar
  • 1/3 cup ranch dressing
  • Butter or oil

Method

  1. Warm chicken on the griddle and chop bacon.
  2. Lay tortillas on medium heat and cover half with cheese.
  3. Add chicken, bacon, and a light drizzle of ranch.
  4. Fold and cook 2 to 3 minutes per side until crisp.
  5. Slice and serve with extra ranch.

Cajun Chicken & Peppers

Smoky Cajun chicken with charred peppers and onions.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, sliced
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tbsp butter

Method

  1. Season chicken with Cajun seasoning.
  2. Sear chicken over medium-high heat until browned.
  3. Cook peppers and onions beside the chicken.
  4. Combine when chicken reaches 165°F and vegetables are tender-crisp.
  5. Finish with butter.

Greek Lemon Herb Chicken

Chicken with oregano, garlic, lemon, and a quick griddled tomato-onion mix.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken breast or thighs, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced

Method

  1. Toss chicken with oil, oregano, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  2. Sear over medium-high heat until cooked through.
  3. Cook tomatoes and onion beside the chicken until blistered.
  4. Squeeze lemon over everything and toss together.

Sweet Chili Chicken

Sticky sweet-hot chicken with peppers, green onion, and lime.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, chopped
  • 1/2 cup sweet chili sauce
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 lime

Method

  1. Sear chicken over medium-high heat in oil.
  2. Add bell pepper and cook until tender-crisp.
  3. Pour in sweet chili sauce and toss until sticky.
  4. Finish with green onions and lime juice.

Chicken Philly Melts

Thin chicken with onions, mushrooms, peppers, and melted provolone.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken breast, shaved thin
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 6 slices provolone
  • 4 hoagie rolls

Method

  1. Cook vegetables over medium-high heat until browned.
  2. Sear chicken quickly on a hot zone and chop as needed.
  3. Combine and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Top portions with provolone and cover briefly.
  5. Serve in toasted rolls.

BBQ Chicken Bacon Melts

Griddled chicken, smoky barbecue sauce, bacon, cheddar, and toasted sourdough.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 6 slices bacon
  • 1/2 cup BBQ sauce
  • 8 slices cheddar
  • 8 slices sourdough
  • 3 tbsp butter

Method

  1. Cook bacon until crisp and reserve.
  2. Sear chicken until cooked through, then toss with BBQ sauce.
  3. Build sandwiches with cheddar, chicken, and bacon.
  4. Butter the outsides of the bread.
  5. Griddle over medium-low heat until golden and melted.
