Cinnamon Griddled Peaches

Caramelized peaches with butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon.

Ingredients

  • 4 peaches, halved or sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: vanilla ice cream

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium.
  2. Melt butter and place peaches cut-side down.
  3. Cook until browned and softened.
  4. Sprinkle with brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt and toss briefly.
  5. Serve warm.
Dessert · Easy · 15 min

S'mores Quesadillas

Crisp tortillas filled with chocolate and marshmallows for a fast griddle dessert.

Ingredients

  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Crushed graham crackers

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-low.
  2. Fill half of each tortilla with chocolate and marshmallows and fold.
  3. Butter the griddle lightly and cook until golden underneath.
  4. Flip carefully and cook until the filling melts.
  5. Top with crushed graham crackers.
