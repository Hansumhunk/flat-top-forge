Cinnamon Griddled Peaches

Caramelized peaches with butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon.

Ingredients

  • 4 peaches, halved or sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: vanilla ice cream

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium.
  2. Melt butter and place peaches cut-side down.
  3. Cook until browned and softened.
  4. Sprinkle with brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt and toss briefly.
  5. Serve warm.

S'mores Quesadillas

Crisp tortillas filled with chocolate and marshmallows for a fast griddle dessert.

Ingredients

  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Crushed graham crackers

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-low.
  2. Fill half of each tortilla with chocolate and marshmallows and fold.
  3. Butter the griddle lightly and cook until golden underneath.
  4. Flip carefully and cook until the filling melts.
  5. Top with crushed graham crackers.

Caramelized Bananas

Buttery banana slices with a quick brown-sugar crust.

Ingredients

  • 4 firm bananas, sliced lengthwise
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-low.
  2. Melt butter and sprinkle the surface lightly with brown sugar.
  3. Place bananas cut-side down and cook 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Flip carefully, season with cinnamon and salt, and cook 1 minute more.
  5. Serve over ice cream, pancakes, or pound cake.
Dessert · Easy · 15 min

Apple Pie Quesadillas

Warm cinnamon apples and melted cream cheese tucked into crisp tortillas.

Ingredients

  • 2 apples, thinly sliced
  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon

Method

  1. Cook apples with 1 tbsp butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon over medium heat until tender.
  2. Spread cream cheese over half of each tortilla.
  3. Add apples, fold, and butter the outside lightly.
  4. Cook 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Slice and serve warm.

Brown Sugar Griddled Pineapple

Juicy pineapple rings caramelized with brown sugar and lime.

Ingredients

  • 1 pineapple, cut into rings or spears
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 lime

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high.
  2. Brush pineapple with butter and sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Cook 2 to 3 minutes per side until deeply marked and caramelized.
  4. Squeeze lime over the pineapple before serving.

Churro Tortilla Crisps

Buttery flour tortillas toasted crisp and coated in cinnamon sugar.

Ingredients

  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 3 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Optional: chocolate sauce

Method

  1. Mix sugar and cinnamon in a shallow dish.
  2. Cut tortillas into wedges and brush lightly with butter.
  3. Cook over medium heat until crisp and browned on both sides.
  4. Toss immediately in cinnamon sugar.
  5. Serve plain or with chocolate sauce.

Strawberry Cheesecake Quesadillas

Crispy tortillas filled with sweet cream cheese and warm strawberries.

Ingredients

  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup sliced strawberries
  • 2 tbsp butter

Method

  1. Mix cream cheese, powdered sugar, and vanilla.
  2. Spread over half of each tortilla and top with strawberries.
  3. Fold and butter the griddle lightly.
  4. Cook over medium-low heat 2 to 3 minutes per side.
  5. Cool briefly before slicing.

Blueberry Lemon Dessert Pancakes

Fluffy lemon-scented pancakes packed with blueberries.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pancake batter
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Butter for the griddle
  • Powdered sugar for serving

Method

  1. Stir lemon zest and sugar into the batter.
  2. Heat the griddle to medium-low and butter lightly.
  3. Pour pancakes and scatter blueberries over each.
  4. Flip when bubbles form and cook through.
  5. Dust with powdered sugar.

Chocolate Hazelnut Banana Roll-Ups

Golden tortillas rolled around banana and chocolate-hazelnut spread.

Ingredients

  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 1/2 cup chocolate-hazelnut spread
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Powdered sugar, optional

Method

  1. Spread each tortilla with chocolate-hazelnut spread.
  2. Add banana slices and roll tightly.
  3. Cook seam-side down over medium-low heat in butter.
  4. Rotate until all sides are golden and filling is warm.
  5. Slice and dust with powdered sugar.
Dessert · Easy · 15 min

Griddled Pound Cake Berry Stacks

Butter-toasted pound cake layered with berries and whipped cream.

Ingredients

  • 8 slices pound cake
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cups mixed berries
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Whipped cream

Method

  1. Toss berries with sugar and let stand 10 minutes.
  2. Heat the griddle to medium-low and melt butter.
  3. Toast pound cake 1 to 2 minutes per side until golden.
  4. Layer with berries and whipped cream.

Cinnamon Roll Pancake Swirls

Pancakes with a buttery cinnamon-sugar spiral and cream-cheese drizzle.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pancake batter
  • 3 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/3 cup cream cheese glaze

Method

  1. Mix melted butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon.
  2. Pour pancake batter onto a medium-low griddle.
  3. Pipe or drizzle a thin cinnamon swirl over each pancake.
  4. Flip carefully when bubbles form and cook through.
  5. Finish with cream-cheese glaze.
Dessert · Easy · 15 min

Peanut Butter Chocolate Griddle Sandwiches

Crisp buttery bread filled with warm peanut butter and melted chocolate.

Ingredients

  • 8 slices sandwich bread
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • Pinch of flaky salt, optional

Method

  1. Spread peanut butter on four bread slices and add chocolate chips.
  2. Top with remaining bread and butter the outsides.
  3. Cook over medium-low heat 2 to 3 minutes per side.
  4. Remove when golden and the chocolate is melted.
  5. Rest 1 minute before slicing.
