Loaded breakfast hash

Crispy potatoes, sausage, peppers, onions, eggs, and cheese built in zones so breakfast finishes together instead of becoming six separate emergencies.

Breakfast · Easy · About 25 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb diced potatoes
  • 8 oz breakfast sausage
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, black pepper, and garlic powder

Griddle setup

Run one zone at medium-high for the potatoes and sausage and keep another zone closer to medium for the eggs. If your potatoes are raw, cut them small and evenly so they brown before the rest of civilization has moved on to lunch.

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and spread on the oil.
  2. Add potatoes in a single layer, season, and cook 10 to 12 minutes, turning occasionally until browned and tender.
  3. Add sausage, peppers, and onions. Chop the sausage as it cooks and continue until no pink remains.
  4. Combine the hash, then create four small wells.
  5. Crack an egg into each well. Cover briefly if you want the whites to set faster.
  6. Sprinkle with cheddar and serve when the eggs reach your preferred doneness.

Make it easier

Par-cooked potatoes shorten the cook and make timing easier. Leftover roasted potatoes also work well. Hot sauce, salsa, green onions, or jalapeños can be added at the end without changing the core method.
