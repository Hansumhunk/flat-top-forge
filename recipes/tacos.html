Taco recipes

Tacos · Easy · 25 min

Chicken Fajita Tacos

Seasoned chicken, peppers, and onions tucked into hot griddled tortillas.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sliced chicken
  • 2 bell peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 8 small tortillas
  • Oil and lime

Method

  1. Cook chicken over medium-high heat with oil and seasoning.
  2. Cook peppers and onions until browned and tender-crisp.
  3. Combine and squeeze with lime.
  4. Warm tortillas on the griddle and fill.

Smash Burger Tacos

Ground beef smashed directly onto tortillas for crispy beef-and-cheese tacos.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • Pickles, onions, and burger sauce

Method

  1. Press a thin layer of beef onto one side of each tortilla.
  2. Place beef-side down on a hot griddle and smash briefly.
  3. Cook until browned, flip, and add cheese.
  4. Fold and finish with pickles, onion, and sauce.

Steak Street Tacos

Hard-seared chopped steak with onion, cilantro, lime, and warm corn tortillas.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb skirt or flank steak, chopped
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1/2 onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • 2 limes
  • Salt, pepper, and garlic powder

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to high and oil lightly.
  2. Season steak and sear in a single layer until browned.
  3. Chop finer on the griddle if desired.
  4. Warm tortillas on a cooler zone.
  5. Fill with steak, onion, cilantro, and lime.

Chorizo Potato Tacos

Crispy potatoes and spicy chorizo with melted cheese and salsa.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Mexican chorizo
  • 1 lb diced par-cooked potatoes
  • 8 small tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Crisp potatoes over medium-high heat in oil.
  2. Cook chorizo beside them, breaking it into crumbles.
  3. Combine once the chorizo is fully cooked.
  4. Warm tortillas and add cheese.
  5. Fill with chorizo potatoes and salsa.

Bacon Egg Breakfast Tacos

Soft scrambled eggs, crisp bacon, cheese, and warm tortillas straight from the flat top.

Ingredients

  • 8 slices bacon
  • 6 eggs
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • Hot sauce, optional

Method

  1. Cook bacon until crisp and chop it.
  2. Scramble eggs softly in a little bacon fat on a medium-low zone.
  3. Warm tortillas on the griddle.
  4. Fill with eggs, bacon, cheddar, and green onions.
  5. Add hot sauce if desired.

Chili Lime Shrimp Tacos

Quick-seared shrimp with chili, lime, crunchy slaw, and warm tortillas.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb peeled shrimp
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 lime
  • 8 small tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded slaw mix
  • 1/3 cup crema or sour cream

Method

  1. Toss shrimp with oil and chili powder.
  2. Sear over medium-high heat 1 to 2 minutes per side until opaque.
  3. Squeeze lime over the shrimp.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill with slaw, shrimp, and crema.

Korean Beef Tacos

Sweet-savory ground beef with sesame, green onion, and crunchy slaw.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 8 tortillas
  • Slaw and green onions

Method

  1. Brown beef over medium-high heat and chop finely.
  2. Add soy sauce, sweetener, garlic, and sesame oil.
  3. Cook until the sauce clings to the beef.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill with beef, slaw, and green onions.

Crispy Carnitas Tacos

Pre-cooked pulled pork crisped hard on the griddle with citrus and spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb cooked pulled pork
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1 orange or lime
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • Diced onion and cilantro

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and oil lightly.
  2. Spread pork thin and leave it untouched until crisp underneath.
  3. Flip sections, season with cumin, and crisp again.
  4. Squeeze citrus over the pork.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with onion and cilantro.

Griddled Veggie Fajita Tacos

Charred peppers, onions, zucchini, corn, cheese, and lime.

Ingredients

  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 cup corn
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 8 tortillas

Method

  1. Toss vegetables with oil and fajita seasoning.
  2. Cook over medium-high heat in a single layer.
  3. Let edges char before tossing.
  4. Warm tortillas on a cooler zone.
  5. Fill with vegetables and finish with lime and cheese if desired.

Blackened Fish Tacos

Spiced white fish seared on the flat top with lime slaw and crema.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb firm white fish
  • 1 tbsp blackening seasoning
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 8 tortillas
  • 2 cups slaw mix
  • 1 lime
  • 1/3 cup crema

Method

  1. Pat fish dry and season with blackening seasoning.
  2. Sear over medium-high heat in oil until browned and flaky.
  3. Break into taco-sized pieces.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill with fish, slaw, crema, and lime.
Tacos · Easy · 20 min

Cheesesteak Tacos

Shaved beef, onions, peppers, and provolone folded into toasted tortillas.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb shaved beef
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 8 slices provolone
  • Oil, salt, and pepper

Method

  1. Cook onions and peppers over medium-high heat.
  2. Sear beef on a hot zone and season.
  3. Combine beef and vegetables.
  4. Add filling and provolone to tortillas.
  5. Fold and toast both sides until the cheese melts.
Tacos · Easy · 20 min

Honey Chipotle Chicken Tacos

Sweet-smoky chicken glazed on the griddle and finished with lime and avocado.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, chopped
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp chipotle sauce
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 8 tortillas
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Sear chicken over medium-high heat until browned and nearly cooked through.
  2. Mix honey, chipotle sauce, and lime juice.
  3. Pour over chicken and toss until sticky and fully cooked.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill with chicken and avocado.
