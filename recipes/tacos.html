Chicken Fajita Tacos

Seasoned chicken, peppers, and onions tucked into hot griddled tortillas.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sliced chicken
  • 2 bell peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 8 small tortillas
  • Oil and lime

Method

  1. Cook chicken over medium-high heat with oil and seasoning.
  2. Cook peppers and onions until browned and tender-crisp.
  3. Combine and squeeze with lime.
  4. Warm tortillas on the griddle and fill.
Tacos · Easy · 20 min

Smash Burger Tacos

Ground beef smashed directly onto tortillas for crispy beef-and-cheese tacos.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • Pickles, onions, and burger sauce

Method

  1. Press a thin layer of beef onto one side of each tortilla.
  2. Place beef-side down on a hot griddle and smash briefly.
  3. Cook until browned, flip, and add cheese.
  4. Fold and finish with pickles, onion, and sauce.
