Charred Zucchini, Peppers & Onions

A colorful all-purpose vegetable mix with caramelized edges for sides, tacos, bowls, and sandwiches.

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high.
  2. Toss vegetables with oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread in one layer and leave them undisturbed for 2 to 3 minutes to develop color.
  4. Toss and continue cooking until tender-crisp with dark edges, about 8 to 10 minutes total.

Garlic Butter Mushrooms

Deeply browned mushrooms finished with butter, garlic, and herbs instead of being steamed into sad little sponges.

Ingredients

  • 16 oz mushrooms, halved
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and add the oil.
  2. Place mushrooms cut-side down and leave them alone 3 to 4 minutes until browned.
  3. Toss and cook another 3 to 4 minutes until tender.
  4. Lower the heat slightly, add butter and garlic, and toss for 30 to 60 seconds.
  5. Finish with parsley, salt, and pepper.

Griddle Street Corn

Charred corn tossed with creamy lime sauce, Cotija, and chili seasoning for an elote-inspired side.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups corn kernels, fresh or thawed frozen
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup crumbled Cotija cheese
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and coat lightly with oil.
  2. Spread the corn into a thin layer and cook 6 to 8 minutes, tossing occasionally, until blistered and browned.
  3. Move the corn to a bowl or cooler zone.
  4. Mix with mayonnaise, lime juice, chili powder, and a pinch of salt.
  5. Top with Cotija and cilantro before serving.

Crispy Parmesan Smashed Potatoes

Par-cooked baby potatoes smashed flat, crisped hard on the griddle, and finished with Parmesan.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb baby potatoes, boiled until fork-tender
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chopped parsley, optional

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and spread on the oil.
  2. Place cooked potatoes on the surface and gently smash each to about 1/2-inch thick.
  3. Season with garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  4. Cook 4 to 5 minutes per side until deeply crisp.
  5. Sprinkle with Parmesan during the final minute and garnish with parsley.

Lemon Garlic Asparagus

Tender-crisp asparagus with browned tips, garlic butter, and a bright squeeze of lemon.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb asparagus, trimmed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high.
  2. Toss asparagus with oil, salt, and pepper and place in a single layer.
  3. Cook 6 to 8 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned and tender-crisp.
  4. Add butter and garlic during the last minute and toss to coat.
  5. Squeeze lemon over the asparagus just before serving.

Balsamic Brussels Sprouts

Halved Brussels sprouts with crisp faces, tender centers, and a sweet-tangy balsamic glaze.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium and oil the surface.
  2. Place sprouts cut-side down and cook 5 to 6 minutes without disturbing them.
  3. Toss, season, and cook another 5 to 7 minutes until nearly tender.
  4. Mix balsamic vinegar and honey, pour over the sprouts, and toss for 1 to 2 minutes until glazed.
  5. Serve immediately.

Cajun Corn, Peppers & Onions

Sweet corn and colorful peppers hit with Cajun seasoning for a fast side with actual personality.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups corn kernels
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 to 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tbsp butter

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and add the oil.
  2. Cook peppers and onions for 3 to 4 minutes until they begin to soften.
  3. Add corn and Cajun seasoning and spread everything into an even layer.
  4. Cook another 5 to 7 minutes, tossing occasionally, until charred in spots.
  5. Finish with butter and serve hot.

Garlic Green Beans with Toasted Almonds

Blistered green beans with garlic, butter, and crunchy toasted almonds.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh green beans, trimmed
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup sliced almonds
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and add oil.
  2. Cook green beans 7 to 9 minutes, tossing occasionally, until blistered and tender-crisp.
  3. Move them aside and toast almonds on a dry section for 1 to 2 minutes.
  4. Add butter and garlic to the beans and cook 30 seconds.
  5. Toss with toasted almonds, salt, and pepper.

Southwest Sweet Potato Hash

Sweet potatoes, peppers, onions, and smoky seasoning browned into a hearty side or meatless breakfast base.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sweet potatoes, peeled and diced small
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium and add oil.
  2. Add sweet potatoes, season, and cook 10 to 12 minutes, turning every few minutes.
  3. Add a small splash of water and cover for 2 minutes if the centers need help softening.
  4. Add peppers and onions and cook another 5 to 7 minutes.
  5. Serve when the potatoes are tender inside and browned outside.

Griddled Cabbage Steaks

Thick cabbage wedges seared until smoky, tender, and caramelized around the edges.

Ingredients

  • 1 small green cabbage
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp butter, optional

Method

  1. Cut cabbage into thick wedges, keeping part of the core attached so each piece holds together.
  2. Brush with oil and season both sides.
  3. Heat the griddle to medium and cook wedges 5 to 7 minutes per side.
  4. Add a splash of water beside the cabbage and cover briefly if needed to soften the centers.
  5. Finish with butter if desired and serve once deeply browned.

Garlic Parmesan Broccoli

Broccoli florets steamed just enough to tenderize, then seared with garlic and Parmesan.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp water
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and add oil.
  2. Add broccoli and cook 2 to 3 minutes until the florets begin to brown.
  3. Add the water beside the broccoli and cover with a dome for 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Remove the dome and cook until excess moisture evaporates.
  5. Add garlic for the final 30 seconds, then toss with Parmesan, salt, and pepper.

Mediterranean Griddle Vegetables

Zucchini, tomato, peppers, onion, and herbs finished with feta and lemon for a bright all-purpose side.

Ingredients

  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 yellow squash, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high.
  2. Toss zucchini, squash, pepper, and onion with oil, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Cook 7 to 9 minutes, allowing the vegetables to brown before tossing.
  4. Add cherry tomatoes during the final 2 to 3 minutes so they blister without completely collapsing.
  5. Finish with feta and fresh lemon juice.
