Charred Zucchini, Peppers & Onions

A simple vegetable mix for sides, tacos, and bowls.

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchini, sliced
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high.
  2. Toss vegetables with oil and seasoning.
  3. Spread in one layer and let them brown before stirring.
  4. Cook until tender-crisp with dark edges.
Vegetables · Easy · 15 min

Garlic Butter Mushrooms

Deeply browned mushrooms finished with garlic and butter.

Ingredients

  • 16 oz mushrooms, halved
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Salt, pepper, parsley

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and add oil.
  2. Spread mushrooms cut-side down and leave them alone until browned.
  3. Toss and cook until tender.
  4. Add butter and garlic at the end and season to taste.
