Loaded Breakfast Hash

Crispy potatoes, peppers, onions, sausage, eggs, and cheese cooked together on one hot surface.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb diced potatoes
  • 8 oz breakfast sausage
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, black pepper, and garlic powder

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and spread on the oil.
  2. Add potatoes in an even layer, season, and cook 10 to 12 minutes, turning occasionally until browned and tender.
  3. Add sausage, peppers, and onions. Chop the sausage as it cooks and continue until no pink remains.
  4. Combine everything, then make four wells and crack an egg into each one.
  5. Cover briefly to help the whites set, then sprinkle with cheddar and serve when the eggs reach your preferred doneness.

Bacon, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Smash

A toasted breakfast sandwich with crisp bacon, griddle eggs, and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 4 sandwich rolls or English muffins
  • 8 slices bacon
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cook bacon over medium heat until crisp and move it to a cooler zone.
  2. Butter and toast the cut sides of the rolls until golden.
  3. Crack eggs onto the griddle, break the yolks if desired, and season.
  4. Flip the eggs, top each with cheese, and cook until melted.
  5. Stack egg, cheese, and bacon on the toasted rolls and serve hot.

Sausage, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Quesadillas

Golden tortillas stuffed with seasoned sausage, fluffy eggs, and plenty of melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz breakfast sausage
  • 6 large eggs
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar or Monterey Jack
  • 2 tbsp butter or oil
  • 2 tbsp sliced green onions, optional
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium and cook the sausage, chopping it into small crumbles.
  2. Move sausage to a cooler zone. Melt a little butter and scramble the eggs softly, seasoning to taste.
  3. Lay tortillas on the griddle and cover half of each with cheese, sausage, eggs, and green onions.
  4. Fold the tortillas over and cook 2 to 3 minutes per side until crisp and deeply golden.
  5. Cut into wedges and serve with salsa or hot sauce.

Cinnamon-Vanilla French Toast

Thick slices of bread griddled in butter until caramelized outside and custardy in the center.

Ingredients

  • 8 thick slices brioche or Texas toast
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • 3 tbsp butter

Method

  1. Whisk eggs, milk, honey, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt in a shallow dish.
  2. Heat the griddle to medium and melt a thin layer of butter.
  3. Dip each bread slice briefly in the custard, coating both sides without soaking it to pieces.
  4. Cook 3 to 4 minutes per side until browned and the center is set.
  5. Serve with maple syrup, berries, powdered sugar, or a little extra butter.

Buttermilk Griddle Pancakes

Fluffy diner-style pancakes with crisp buttery edges and soft centers.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 4 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Butter or oil for the griddle

Method

  1. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Whisk buttermilk, eggs, melted butter, and vanilla separately, then combine with the dry ingredients just until mixed.
  3. Heat the griddle to medium-low and grease lightly.
  4. Pour about 1/4 cup batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form and the edges begin to set.
  5. Flip once and cook another 1 to 2 minutes until golden and cooked through.

Crispy Hash Browns & Over-Easy Eggs

Crunchy shredded potatoes with buttery eggs for the purest possible diner breakfast move.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb frozen shredded hash browns, thawed
  • 4 large eggs
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Squeeze excess moisture from the hash browns with a clean towel.
  2. Heat the griddle to medium-high and spread on the oil.
  3. Add potatoes in a thin layer, season, and press gently. Leave them alone 5 to 7 minutes so a crust forms.
  4. Flip in sections and cook another 4 to 6 minutes until crisp on both sides.
  5. Lower one zone to medium, melt butter, and cook the eggs over-easy. Serve them over or beside the hash browns.

Steak & Egg Breakfast Burritos

Seared steak, scrambled eggs, potatoes, cheese, and salsa wrapped in a toasted tortilla.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz sirloin or flat iron steak, thinly sliced
  • 6 large eggs
  • 2 cups diced cooked potatoes
  • 1 cup shredded pepper Jack or cheddar
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika

Method

  1. Heat one side of the griddle to high and another to medium.
  2. Oil the hot zone and sear the steak quickly, seasoning as it cooks. Remove when browned and just cooked through.
  3. Crisp the potatoes on the medium-high zone, then scramble the eggs on the medium zone.
  4. Fill tortillas with steak, potatoes, eggs, cheese, and salsa. Fold tightly into burritos.
  5. Place seam-side down on the griddle and toast 1 to 2 minutes per side until golden.

Southwest Chorizo Breakfast Tacos

Spicy chorizo, eggs, peppers, onions, cheese, and warm tortillas made for breakfast with some backbone.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Mexican chorizo
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/2 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 small onion, diced
  • 8 small flour or corn tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • Fresh cilantro or sliced green onions, optional

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium and cook the chorizo until browned and fully cooked.
  2. Add peppers and onions and cook until softened.
  3. Move the mixture aside and scramble the eggs on a lightly greased section of the griddle.
  4. Warm the tortillas 20 to 30 seconds per side.
  5. Fill with eggs, chorizo mixture, cheese, salsa, and cilantro or green onions.

Ham, Egg & Cheese Griddle Melts

A buttery breakfast melt with seared ham, folded eggs, and gooey cheese between crisp bread.

Ingredients

  • 8 slices sandwich bread or sourdough
  • 8 oz sliced ham
  • 4 large eggs
  • 8 slices cheddar, Swiss, or American cheese
  • 3 tbsp softened butter
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium.
  2. Sear the ham briefly on both sides and move it to a cooler section.
  3. Cook the eggs as thin folded omelets, seasoning lightly.
  4. Butter the outside of the bread. Build each sandwich with cheese, ham, egg, and a second slice of cheese.
  5. Cook 2 to 3 minutes per side, pressing lightly, until the bread is crisp and the cheese melts.

Bacon Breakfast Fried Rice

Day-old rice, bacon, eggs, and green onions transformed into a fast savory breakfast straight off the flat top.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cold cooked rice
  • 6 slices bacon, chopped
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup diced onion
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil, optional
  • Black pepper to taste

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and cook the bacon until crisp.
  2. Push bacon to one side, leaving a little rendered fat on the cooking surface.
  3. Add onion and peas and carrots. Cook 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Add cold rice, spread it out, and let it sear before tossing with soy sauce and bacon.
  5. Scramble the eggs on an open section, then fold them into the rice. Finish with green onions, sesame oil, and black pepper.
