Loaded Breakfast Hash

Crispy potatoes, peppers, onions, sausage, eggs, and cheese cooked on one hot surface.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb diced potatoes
  • 8 oz breakfast sausage
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • Oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and oil lightly.
  2. Cook potatoes until browned and tender, about 10 to 12 minutes.
  3. Add sausage, peppers, and onions and cook through.
  4. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, and cover briefly if desired.
  5. Top with cheese and serve.
Breakfast · Easy · 15 min

Bacon, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Smash

A toasted breakfast sandwich with crisp bacon, griddle eggs, and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 4 sandwich rolls or English muffins
  • 8 slices bacon
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • Butter, salt, and pepper

Method

  1. Cook bacon over medium heat until crisp and move it to a cooler zone.
  2. Butter and toast the cut sides of the rolls.
  3. Crack eggs onto the griddle, break the yolks if desired, and season.
  4. Flip the eggs, add cheese, and cook until melted.
  5. Stack egg, cheese, and bacon on the toasted rolls.
