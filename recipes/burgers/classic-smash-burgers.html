Classic smash burgers

Thin patties, hard contact with hot steel, crisp browned edges, melted cheese, and absolutely no need to turn dinner into a committee meeting.

Burgers · Easy · 15 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional: diced onions, pickles, burger sauce

Griddle setup

Use a high-heat zone for the patties and a slightly cooler area for bun toasting and holding finished burgers. A stiff metal spatula or burger turner makes it much easier to scrape under the crust without leaving half the good part welded to the steel.

Method

  1. Preheat the griddle to high heat.
  2. Divide the beef into four loose balls. Do not compress them into firm patties before cooking.
  3. Place the beef on the hot surface and immediately smash each ball firmly for about 10 seconds.
  4. Season with salt and pepper. Leave the patties alone until the edges turn dark brown and crisp.
  5. Scrape firmly underneath each patty and flip once.
  6. Add cheese and cook just long enough to finish the second side.
  7. Toast the buns on a nearby medium zone and assemble with your preferred toppings.

What makes the crust work

The important variables are dry meat surface, hard contact with the steel, enough heat, and leaving the patty alone long enough to brown. Repeatedly poking, lifting, and moving it is an excellent way to trade crust for anxiety.

Useful pairings

Serve with griddled onions, pickles, fries, hash browns, or charred vegetables. For a double burger, use smaller beef balls and stack two thin patties instead of making one thick one.
