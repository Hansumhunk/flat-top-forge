Griddle Fried Rice

Cold rice, eggs, vegetables, butter, and soy sauce browned fast on the griddle.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cold cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup peas and carrots
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Heat griddle to medium-high and add oil.
  2. Scramble eggs on one side and cook vegetables on the other.
  3. Add rice and spread thin for browning.
  4. Mix together with butter and soy sauce.
Hibachi · Medium · 30 min

Hibachi Chicken Dinner

Chicken, zucchini, onion, mushrooms, and fried rice cooked in separate heat zones.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1 zucchini, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 8 oz mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Garlic, oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Cook chicken over medium-high heat until browned and nearly done.
  2. Cook vegetables in a neighboring zone with oil.
  3. Finish chicken with butter, garlic, and soy sauce.
  4. Combine vegetables briefly, then serve with fried rice.
