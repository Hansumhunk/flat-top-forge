Honey garlic chicken

Brown the chicken first, glaze second. The order matters unless caramelized honey welded to steel was somehow the objective.

Chicken · Easy · 20 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Griddle setup

Use medium-high heat for browning and keep a lower-heat zone available before adding the honey mixture. Sugary sauces can burn quickly on a very hot surface, so the glaze belongs at the end.

Method

  1. Mix soy sauce, honey, garlic, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
  2. Lightly oil the griddle and cook the chicken over medium-high heat in a single layer.
  3. Turn and chop as needed until the pieces are browned and nearly cooked through.
  4. Move the chicken to a slightly cooler zone and pour on the sauce.
  5. Toss continuously until the sauce reduces to a shiny coating and the chicken is cooked through.

Serve it with

Fried rice, white rice, griddled zucchini, peppers, onions, broccoli, or a simple cucumber side all work. Green onions or sesame seeds make easy finishing touches if you have them.

Food-safety note: verify chicken is cooked to a safe internal temperature with a food thermometer rather than relying on surface browning alone.
