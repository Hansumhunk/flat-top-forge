Garlic butter steak bites

Small steak pieces mean more browned surface area, faster cooking, and considerably less waiting around pretending patience is a seasoning.

Steak · Medium · 25 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sirloin or strip steak, cut into bite-size cubes
  • 2 tbsp high-heat oil
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional: chopped parsley or thyme

Griddle setup

Run one zone hot enough to sear and keep another zone closer to medium. The hot zone builds crust; the cooler zone gives you somewhere to finish with butter and garlic without turning the garlic into bitter gravel.

Method

  1. Pat the steak dry and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Preheat a high-heat griddle zone and add a thin film of oil.
  3. Spread steak pieces in a single layer with space between them. Work in batches if needed.
  4. Leave the first side alone long enough to brown, then turn the pieces and sear additional sides.
  5. Move the steak to the medium zone when it is close to your preferred doneness.
  6. Add butter and garlic, toss briefly, then remove from the griddle before the garlic burns.

What to serve with it

Potatoes, mushrooms, onions, asparagus, zucchini, rice, or a simple salad all fit. The same method also works as the protein base for steak tacos or a loaded griddle bowl.
