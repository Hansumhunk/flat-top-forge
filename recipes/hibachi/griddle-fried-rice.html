Griddle fried rice

Cold rice, eggs, vegetables, butter, and soy sauce spread across hot steel where fried rice finally gets enough elbow room to behave properly.

Hibachi · Easy · 20 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cold cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup peas and carrots
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • Optional: sliced green onion, chicken, shrimp, or steak

Why cold rice works better

Chilled rice is drier and firmer than freshly cooked rice, so the grains separate and brown more easily instead of turning into one heroic mound of steamed starch.

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and add the oil.
  2. Scramble the eggs on one side of the griddle and cook the vegetables on another.
  3. Add the cold rice and spread it into a relatively thin layer.
  4. Leave portions of the rice in contact with the steel long enough to pick up some browning.
  5. Mix the eggs and vegetables into the rice.
  6. Add butter and soy sauce, toss well, and cook until everything is hot and evenly seasoned.
  7. Fold in cooked protein at the end if using.

Build a full hibachi-style meal

Cook chicken, steak, shrimp, zucchini, onions, or mushrooms in neighboring heat zones, then combine portions at serving time rather than dumping every ingredient into one pile from the beginning.
