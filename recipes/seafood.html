Garlic Butter Shrimp

Quick-seared shrimp finished with garlic, butter, lemon, and black pepper.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb peeled shrimp
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Pat shrimp dry and season.
  2. Heat griddle to medium-high and add oil.
  3. Sear shrimp in a single layer until pink and browned.
  4. Add butter and garlic for the final minute.
  5. Finish with lemon.
Seafood · Easy · 20 min

Blackened Fish Tacos

Seasoned white fish seared on the griddle and tucked into warm tortillas with slaw.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb firm white fish
  • 2 tbsp blackening seasoning
  • 8 tortillas
  • 2 cups slaw mix
  • Lime and crema
  • Oil

Method

  1. Season fish generously.
  2. Sear on a medium-high griddle until browned and cooked through.
  3. Break into large flakes.
  4. Warm tortillas and fill with fish, slaw, lime, and crema.
