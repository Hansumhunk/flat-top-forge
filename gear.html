Build your setup by level
Level 1 · Basic essentials

Essential griddle tools

A standard spatula, long spatula, scraper, tongs, press, dome, three-bottle setup, two thermometer types, and the core tools needed to run a proper cook.

 Level 2 · Upgraded cooking gear

Build a faster workstation

 Level 3 · Specialty gear

Tools for specific cooking styles

Egg rings, pancake dispensers, fish spatulas, taco tools, steaming racks, long cooking forks, and other equipment that earns its place through repeated use.

 Level 4 · Prep & organization

 Before you buy

Accessory compatibility

Check model number, dimensions, mounting geometry, and manufacturer documentation before assuming “universal” means anything useful.

The rule: buy workflow, not clutter

A good accessory should improve control, speed, safety, cleanup, consistency, or organization. Start with the basic kit. Upgrade when repeated cooks expose a bottleneck. Buy specialty tools only when a specific task happens often enough to justify dedicated gear.
