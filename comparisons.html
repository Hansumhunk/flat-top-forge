We compare by use case, not just specification sheets

Cooking area, burner count, dimensions, storage, portability, and accessory support matter differently depending on whether you cook breakfast for two, burgers for a family, or food for a crowd. Comparison pages will explain the tradeoffs instead of declaring one model universally “best.”

Research standard

Manufacturer specifications and documentation are treated as primary sources for dimensions and stated features. Customer feedback can help identify recurring ownership issues, but it is not presented as controlled testing. Hands-on experience will be labeled when it exists.
