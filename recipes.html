Breakfast · Easy · 20 min

Loaded Breakfast Hash

Crispy potatoes, peppers, onions, sausage, eggs, and cheese all cooked on one hot surface.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb diced potatoes
  • 8 oz breakfast sausage
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • Oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and oil lightly.
  2. Cook potatoes first until browned and tender, about 10 to 12 minutes.
  3. Add sausage, peppers, and onions. Cook until the sausage is done and vegetables soften.
  4. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, and cover briefly if you want the tops set faster.
  5. Top with cheese and serve hot.
Burgers · Easy · 15 min

Classic Smash Burgers

Thin, deeply browned patties with crisp edges, melted cheese, and a toasted bun.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional: diced onions, pickles, burger sauce

Method

  1. Preheat the griddle to high heat.
  2. Divide beef into four loose balls. Do not pack them tightly.
  3. Place on the hot surface and smash hard for about 10 seconds.
  4. Season, cook until the edges brown deeply, then scrape and flip.
  5. Add cheese, toast the buns beside the patties, and assemble.
Dinner · Medium · 25 min

Garlic Butter Steak Bites

High-heat seared steak pieces finished with butter, garlic, and herbs.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sirloin or strip steak, cubed
  • 2 tbsp high-heat oil
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt, pepper, optional parsley

Method

  1. Pat the steak dry and season generously.
  2. Heat one griddle zone to high and oil lightly.
  3. Sear steak in a single layer without crowding.
  4. Turn once browned, then move to a cooler zone.
  5. Add butter and garlic, toss briefly, and remove before the garlic burns.
Chicken · Easy · 20 min

Honey Garlic Chicken

Sweet-savory chicken with a sticky griddle glaze that works well over rice or vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Method

  1. Mix soy sauce, honey, garlic, and sesame oil.
  2. Cook chicken over medium-high heat until browned and nearly done.
  3. Pour on the sauce and keep the chicken moving.
  4. Cook until the sauce reduces and coats the chicken.
Rice · Easy · 20 min

Griddle Fried Rice

A fast way to turn cold leftover rice into a full meal without dirtying half the kitchen, a rare victory for civilization.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cold cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Optional: chicken, shrimp, green onion

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high and add oil.
  2. Scramble eggs on one side and cook vegetables on the other.
  3. Add cold rice and spread it thin for better browning.
  4. Mix everything together with butter and soy sauce.
  5. Add cooked protein if using and heat through.
Vegetables · Easy · 15 min

Charred Zucchini, Peppers & Onions

A simple griddle vegetable mix that works as a side, taco filling, or base for chicken and rice bowls.

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchini, sliced
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder
  • Optional: lemon juice or fajita seasoning

Method

  1. Heat the griddle to medium-high.
  2. Toss vegetables with oil and seasoning.
  3. Spread in a single layer and let them brown before stirring.
  4. Cook until tender-crisp with dark edges, about 8 to 10 minutes.
