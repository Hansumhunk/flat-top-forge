How the library grows

Each category can hold recipe after recipe

The category pages are built so new recipes can be added without changing the overall site structure. As the library grows, individual recipes can also be split into their own pages for better search visibility, easier linking, and room for photos, equipment recommendations, temperatures, timing charts, and affiliate links.
