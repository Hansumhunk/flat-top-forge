How to make a crispy cheese skirt

Put the cheese on the steel first, let it brown, then attach the food to it. This is what happens when dairy is finally given structural responsibilities.

The basic method

  1. Use a medium to medium-high zone rather than the hottest part of the griddle.
  2. Sprinkle a thin, even layer of shredded cheese directly onto the clean surface.
  3. Let it melt and continue cooking until the edges darken and the underside firms up.
  4. Place the burger, tortilla, or sandwich edge onto the still-soft center of the cheese.
  5. Wait until the skirt releases cleanly, then scrape underneath with a stiff spatula.

Cheeses that work well

Cheddar, Monterey Jack, pepper Jack, and low-moisture mozzarella can all work. Very wet cheese tends to steam more and crisp less efficiently.

The important cue is release

If the cheese still feels glued to the steel, it may not be browned enough yet. Once enough moisture cooks away and the bottom crisps, a good scraper should lift it much more cleanly.
