How to steam-melt cheese fast

The dome traps steam and heat around the food so the cheese melts quickly without forcing the burger, chicken, or sandwich to sit on hard heat forever.

The method

  1. Cook the food until it is nearly at the point where you want to remove it.
  2. Add the cheese.
  3. Move the food to medium or medium-low heat if the sear is already complete.
  4. Place a melting dome over the food.
  5. Add a very small splash of water to the griddle beside the food, not directly on it, then cover immediately.
  6. Lift the dome as soon as the cheese has melted.

Why it works

The enclosed steam transfers heat around the cheese quickly. Because you can move the food off the hottest part of the griddle first, you get melted cheese without needlessly extending the hard sear.

Equipment note: use water only where appropriate for your griddle and follow the manufacturer's cooking and care instructions. Keep your hands clear of escaping steam when lifting the dome.
