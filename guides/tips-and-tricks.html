Guides / Tips & Tricks

Tips, tricks & food techniques

The little moves that make a noticeable difference: better crust, smarter heat control, cleaner timing, and fewer moments where dinner begins negotiating with the smoke alarm.

Burgers & cheese
Smash burgers

Get crispy, lacy burger edges

Use loose beef, a very hot surface, one decisive smash, and a clean scrape for maximum crust.

 Cheese trick

Build a crispy cheese skirt

Brown cheese directly on the griddle, attach it to a burger or taco, then lift the whole thing as one glorious sheet of dairy engineering.

 Melting

Steam-melt cheese fast

Use a dome and a tiny amount of water beside the food to melt cheese quickly without keeping the protein on high heat forever.
Breakfast & potatoes
Breakfast

Diner-crispy hash browns

Dry the potatoes, spread them thin, use enough fat, and stop touching them long enough to let a crust form.
Steak, rice & vegetables
Steak

Sear zone + butter zone

Build crust first, then move to gentler heat before adding butter, garlic, and herbs.

 Rice

Restaurant-style fried rice

Cold rice, separated grains, high heat, multiple zones, and sauce added late enough that the rice still fries.

 Vegetables

Char vegetables instead of steaming them

Dry surfaces, enough heat, breathing room, and delayed stirring help vegetables brown instead of collapse into sadness.

 Hibachi

Time a full hibachi meal

Use prep order, heat zones, holding space, and a deliberate sequence so rice, vegetables, and proteins finish together.

These are technique guides, not separate recipes

Each page focuses on one repeatable skill that can improve several recipes. The trick pages link back into recipes, core how-tos, gear, and troubleshooting so the Guides section stays organized instead of breeding categories for sport.
