Compatibility checks

“It says universal” is not the same thing as “it fits.” This section is for model numbers, dimensions, mounting points, and the deeply human experience of discovering an accessory is half an inch wrong.

What we check
Weather protection

Covers & lids

Replacement

Cooktops & parts

Model identifiers, dimensions, attachment points, and manufacturer part references where available.

Before you buy

Fit-check method

Compare exact model number first, then dimensions, then mounting geometry. Product-title similarity comes last.

Compatibility pages will show the evidence

When a fit claim comes from a manufacturer, manual, specification sheet, or retailer listing, the source should be identified. When fit is inferred from dimensions rather than explicitly documented, that should be labeled as an inference rather than quietly promoted to fact.
