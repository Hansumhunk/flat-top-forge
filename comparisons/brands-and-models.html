Flat-top griddle brands & model families

A working directory of the major griddle lineups worth comparing. The goal is not to crown one universal winner. It is to put comparable machines into the same ring instead of comparing a tabletop griddle to a patio-sized four-burner monster and acting surprised.

Research snapshot · September 2026

Model lineups change, retailer bundles come and go, and manufacturers occasionally rename things because apparently hot steel also needs branding strategy. This directory tracks the major model families we found during current manufacturer research. Exact dimensions, burner counts, cooking area, pricing, warranty, and availability will be verified again from manufacturer documentation on each future head-to-head page.

How we should compare them

The useful comparison is rarely “Brand A versus Brand B” in the abstract. The useful comparison is two or more machines that solve the same problem at roughly the same scale. That means the directory feeds several comparison classes.

Full-size mainstream

Large family griddles

Blackstone 36-class, Weber Slate 36, Traeger IronTop 4-Burner, Camp Chef Gridiron 36, Pit Boss Ultimate 4/5-burner, HALO Elite4B, and Nexgrill Daytona 36-class models.

Mid-size

28–30 inch class

Blackstone 28-class, Weber Slate 28/30, Traeger IronTop 2-Burner, Pit Boss mid-size models, Nexgrill Daytona 28/30, and Cuisinart 28-inch.

Portable

Tabletop & compact

Blackstone 17/22, Weber Slate 17/22, HALO Elite1B, Pit Boss tabletop units, Nexgrill portable models, and other compact platforms.

Premium

Higher-end platforms

HALO Elite models, Traeger Flatrock, premium Weber Slate configurations, and higher-spec Blackstone families where equipment and pricing actually overlap.

Alternative fuel

Pellet vs propane

recteq SmokeStone models belong in a separate comparison because pellet-fired flat tops operate differently from conventional propane griddles.

Hybrid

Grill + griddle systems

Grilla Grills Primate and Royal Gourmet combination units deserve their own category because the buyer is choosing versatility as much as flat-top performance.

Editorial rule

Manufacturer test results may be useful evidence, but they are labeled as manufacturer claims. Flat Top Forge will not quietly transform a company's own benchmark into “independent testing.” Current-model status and exact specifications should be rechecked before any head-to-head article is published.
