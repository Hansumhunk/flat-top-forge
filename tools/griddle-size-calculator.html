How the calculator thinks

This is a planning heuristic, not a manufacturer specification. It gives extra space for larger groups, multi-component meals, and cooks who want fewer batches. Portability pulls the recommendation toward smaller footprints when the other answers allow it.

What the ranges mean

RangeUsually fitsTradeoff
17–22 inch class1–3 people, camping, tailgating, simple mealsEasy to move, less room for multi-zone cooking
28 inch class2–5 people, ordinary family meals, moderate batchingGood middle ground between space and footprint
30–32 inch class4–7 people, mixed meals, more simultaneous cookingMore work space with less portability
36 inch and larger5+ people, entertaining, full breakfasts, hibachi-style cooksMaximum room, largest storage and fuel footprint

Nominal width does not equal exact cooking area. Compare actual cooktop dimensions and specifications before buying.
