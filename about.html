About Flat Top Forge

Why this site exists, and how we research what we publish.

Why we built this

Most griddle content online is either a generic "10 best grills" roundup written for a general audience, or a fan page built entirely around one brand's promotional content. We wanted something in between: a reference site that treats flat-top cooking as a real hobby with real technical questions — what fits what, what's actually worth buying, what to cook, and what to do when something goes wrong.

How we research

We build our comparisons and compatibility pages from manufacturer specifications, published documentation, pricing data, warranty terms, and publicly available customer feedback. Where we haven't personally cooked on or tested a product, we say so directly — we don't imply hands-on testing we haven't done. When we have tested something ourselves, that will be stated plainly on the page.

How the site makes money

Some links on this site are affiliate links, meaning we may earn a small commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. This never changes what we recommend. Full details are on our affiliate disclosure page.

Flat Top Forge is an independent site. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Blackstone Products or any griddle manufacturer.
