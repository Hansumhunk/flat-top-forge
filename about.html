About Flat Top Forge

A practical reference site for people who cook on flat-top griddles and want specific answers without promotional fog.

Why we built this

Flat-top owners ask very specific questions: Will this accessory fit my model? Why is my surface sticky? What temperature range should I use? Which size makes sense? What can I cook besides burgers? Flat Top Forge is being built around those real questions instead of generic roundup content.

What the site covers

Cook

Recipes, heat control, technique, seasoning, cleaning, and maintenance.

Choose

Gear guides, model comparisons, compatibility checks, and accessory research.

Solve

Troubleshooting, charts, calculators, and reference tools designed for repeat use.

How we research

We prioritize manufacturer specifications, manuals, published documentation, warranty information, pricing data, and clearly labeled customer feedback. Where we have not personally cooked on or tested a product, we say so directly. Where something has been tested firsthand, that will be stated plainly.
